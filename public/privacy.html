Privacy Policy

ifoundit · Last updated 2026-09-21

ifoundit is a cosmetic skincare assistant. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice or diagnoses.

Photos

When you run an analysis, the three selfies you take are sent over an encrypted connection to our analysis service, which forwards them to OpenAI's vision API to score visible skin concerns. We do not store your photos on our servers, and under OpenAI's API data-usage policy they are not used to train models. Photos are discarded once the report is generated.

Reports and answers

Your skin reports, concern selections, lifestyle answers and budget are stored only on your device so you can track progress over time. You can delete them at any time from the Progress screen. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Service logs

To keep the service reliable and within usage limits, our analysis service records the time, status, duration and token usage of each request together with a one-way hash of your IP address. Logs never contain photos, reports or your raw IP address.

Product links

Product recommendations link to third-party retailers. Their sites are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not receive information about what you view or buy there.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Children

ifoundit is not intended for children under 13.
